Course Expectations

Attendance & Daily Responsibilities

Regular and daily attendance is required. You must log in to the course every school day and complete the scheduled work.

Every day, you are expected to:

  • Read announcements I have posted since your last login.
  • Check the calendar for daily lessons and assignments.
  • Complete all work (graded and non-graded) before or on the due date.

Asynchronous Students: You are not required to attend live sessions but must watch the session recordings daily. Recordings and class slides are available each day at 3:30 PM.

If you are absent:

  • You are responsible for watching the class recording and completing any missed work.
  • For a missed quiz or test, you must email me the day you return. If you do not contact me in a timely manner, the quiz or test will not be reopened.
  • Extra time will be given to students with extended or consecutive absences.

Communication with Teacher and Classmates

  • In-Class Communication: Be aware that anything posted in class chat or discussion boards can be seen by the entire class. Use school-appropriate language and respect the privacy of others.
  • Prohibited Conduct: Flaming, spamming, bullying, or other unwanted contact will be considered a breach of this policy and may result in disciplinary action. The teacher will never ask you to publicly share your private contact information with other students.

Grades & Submissions

Important Dates

  • Due Date: The date an assignment is expected to be submitted. Turn in work by the end of this day.
  • Lock Date: The last possible date to submit an assignment. No work will be accepted after this date.

Grading Policy

  • Quizzes: You get two attempts on weekly quizzes. Quizzes are due the same day they are assigned and lock one day later.
  • Unit Tests: You get one attempt. Tests are due the same day they are assigned. They will lock one week later or at the end of the grading period, whichever comes first. This applies to all test sections, including written portions.
  • Written Work: This includes worksheets, written assignments, and discussion boards. Work will not be accepted past the lock date. Depending on the length of the assignment, the amount of time given may vary.

Submitting Work

  • Assignments should be submitted through the Assignments tool in Canvas. Assignments sent via email will not be accepted unless you have made prior arrangements with me.
  • I will grade and return teacher-scored assignments within five school days. If you do not receive a grade by then, please contact me.
  • Once graded, you are expected to open them and read the score and feedback I provide to improve your work on future assignments.

Extra Credit

Extra credit opportunities will only be offered to the entire class. You can only submit extra credit work after all other currently available assignments have been submitted.

Academic Dishonesty

Students are expected to maintain the highest standards of honesty in their work. If you are suspected of academic dishonesty, I will have a conversation with you and your family. Consequences may include redoing the assignment for a reduced grade, receiving a zero (0), or other disciplinary action.

Violations include, but are not limited to:

  • Copying work from others.
  • Plagiarizing work or submitting work completed by another person, including a learning coach.
  • Sharing test questions or answers.
  • Using artificial intelligence (AI) to generate essays or answer questions on assessments.
  • Important Note on AI: Artificial intelligence is becoming more widely used and integrated into various web browsers, search engines, and other online tools. It's important to understand that while AI can be a useful resource and study tool, having AI generated responses or using AI to directly answer questions on any assessment is considered Academic Dishonesty. If you are suspected of submitting AI-generated work, you may be asked to complete the assessment orally with your webcam on to demonstrate your understanding.
